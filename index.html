Our story

It started in our kitchen.

We started with plain Salt & Pepper, got bored quickly, and went looking for flavours that felt like home. Pani Puri came first, then Lemon Chilli, then Peri Peri.

Before selling a single pack, we shared samples with our neighbours at Ganesh Chaturthi, for Bappa's blessings and their honest opinions.
