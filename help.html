Ordering.

How do I place an order?

Pick your flavours and pack sizes in the Shop, add them to your bag and check out. We open WhatsApp with your order typed out. Send the message and we will confirm your delivery.

What pack sizes do you have?

Every flavour comes in a 10 g pack (₹30) and a 25 g pack (₹60). The 10 g pack is a snack for one, great for lunch boxes and snack breaks. The 25 g pack is good for sharing and for gifting as return or puja favours.

Need a different size? Ask us, and we will see what we can do.

What's in the Try all four set?

One 10 g pack of each flavour: Pani Puri, Lemon Chilli, Salt & Pepper and Peri Peri. It costs ₹120.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp before your delivery day and we will sort it out.

Delivery & payment.

When do you deliver?

On Saturday and Sunday. You choose the day when you check out.

Is there a delivery fee or a minimum order?

No. Delivery is free right now, and there is no minimum order.

How do I pay?

By UPI, or pay on delivery with cash or UPI at your door. At checkout you can scan our UPI QR or copy the UPI ID. For now the QR is a personal account in our founder’s name (Deepak Gupta), because our business registration and current account are still being set up.

Do you deliver to my area?

Send us your address on WhatsApp and we will let you know.

About the makhana.

What oil do you use?

Pure olive oil. Never palm oil.

How is it made?

We roast makhana at home in small batches, in pure olive oil, and season it with real masalas.

How should I store it?

Keep the pack sealed and in a cool, dry place to keep the crunch. Close the zip after opening.

Bulk & custom orders.

Planning a birthday, Navratri or puja favours, an office event or a house party? Tell us the occasion, the date and how many people or packs you need. We will understand what you need and suggest packs, sizes and flavours that fit.
